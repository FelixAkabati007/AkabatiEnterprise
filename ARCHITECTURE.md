## 1. Executive Definition

Akabati Enterprise is built as a real, database-backed commerce platform supporting a customer-facing storefront and a protected business operations console.

The complete commerce lifecycle is:
1. Discovery and browsing (Electronics and Vehicles).
2. Advanced search, filtering, comparison, and category-specific specs.
3. Customer sign-in or guest checkout.
4. Server/authoritative validation of availability, price, delivery charges, and stock reservations.
5. Multi-channel payment options (Mobile Money - MTN/Telecel, Card/Paystack, or WhatsApp concierge).
6. Order state management through Picking, Packing, Ready for Dispatch, Dispatched, and Delivered.
7. Tracking by order number with status timeline.
8. Business management portal for inventory, orders, customer records, and financial reconciliation.

Refer to full architecture sections for data schema, state transitions, security, and integration rules.
